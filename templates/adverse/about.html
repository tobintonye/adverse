About Advers

We're building the operating layer for physical screens.

Out-of-home advertising has always been analog at heart — bookings by email, spreadsheets for scheduling, and no real proof an ad ever played. Advers turns every screen into a connected, accountable node in a single network.

Our mission

To make buying and running digital out-of-home advertising as simple, transparent, and measurable as buying an ad online — for advertisers of any size, and screen owners of any scale.

Our vision

A world where any physical screen — a storefront display, a roadside billboard, a mall panel — can be discovered, booked, and verified in minutes, not weeks.

The problem we saw

Digital out-of-home is one of the fastest-growing ad channels, but the tooling behind it hasn't caught up. Screen owners manage bookings manually. Advertisers have no reliable way to confirm a campaign actually ran, where, or how often.

Meanwhile, the hardware side — the Android media players actually driving these screens — is often disconnected from the business side entirely, requiring manual content pushes and on-site troubleshooting.

Adverse was built to close that gap: one platform connecting campaign submission, scheduling, delivery, and real device management, end to end.

From the founder

We're not trying to reinvent advertising — we're trying to give an industry that runs the physical world's screens the same operational clarity that digital advertising has had for over a decade. That starts with software that treats every screen as a real, trackable endpoint.

Founder, Adverse
