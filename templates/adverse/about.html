About Us

Bridging the gap between brand vision and public visibility across West Africa. We are the operating system for modern outdoor advertising, built for transparency, impact, and speed.

The Advers Story

Founded in the heart of Lagos, Advers was born out of a simple frustration: advertising on Nigeria's massive billboards was a manual, opaque, and fragmented process.

We saw billboard owners with unsold inventory and advertisers struggling to find available spaces in key hubs like Ikeja, Maitama, and the Port Harcourt GRA. We set out to build the bridge.

By digitizing the inventory of major media owners and creating a transparent booking engine, we've enabled businesses of all sizes—from local startups to multinationals—to light up the streets of Nigeria with their message.
