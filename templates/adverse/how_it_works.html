How Advers works

One platform, two journeys one network.

Whether you're placing a campaign or hosting a screen, Advers handles the entire lifecycle automatically.

SCREEN OWNERS ARE CALLED "AD MANAGERS" INSIDE ADVERSE

01

Submit your campaign

Upload creative, set your budget, and choose target screens or regions in a guided submission flow.

02

Review & approval

Your campaign is checked against screen availability and content guidelines before confirmation.

03

Scheduling

Adverse slots your campaign into the network's schedule, coordinating timing across every screen involved.

04

Live delivery

Your ad is pushed directly to the Android players and plays on schedule no manual intervention required.

05

Reporting & proof-of-play

Get confirmation of exactly when and where your campaign played, in real time.

01

Register your screen

Add your screen's details, location, and availability to the Adverse network in a few minutes.

02

Install the player

Set up the Adverse Android player on your device it handles content sync and playback automatically.

03

Set pricing & availability

Define your ad slots, rates, and open hours. Adverse matches demand to your screen automatically.

04

Receive & display ads

Approved campaigns are delivered and played automatically, with no manual content management.

05

Get paid & track performance

See exactly what played, when, and track your earnings from a simple performance view.
