Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Plain English Summary: We collect only what we need to run the Advers platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Advers Technology Limited ("Advers," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the Advers platform ("Platform"), accessible at advers.ng.

By creating an account or using our services, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. If you disagree, please do not use our Platform. This Policy is part of and subject to our Terms of Service.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Full name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a hash)
  • Profile Information: Company name, job title, business address, and profile photo
  • Campaign Data: Ad creative files, campaign briefs, target locations, and scheduling preferences
  • Media Owner Listings: Asset location, dimensions, photos, technical specs, and pricing
  • Payment Information: Bank account or card details (processed via our payment provider; Advers does not store raw card data)
  • Communications: Messages sent through our contact form, support tickets, or live chat

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and click patterns
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Location Data: General geographic location derived from your IP address
  • Log Files: Server logs, error reports, and performance data
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: See Section 5 for full details

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Identity verification data from KYC/AML service providers where required
  • Publicly available business registration information for media owner verification
  • Analytics data aggregated by third-party analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Creating and managing your account, processing bookings, and executing campaigns
  • Payment Processing: Facilitating secure transactions between advertisers and media owners
  • Platform Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns to improve features, fix bugs, and enhance the user experience
  • Communications: Sending transactional emails (booking confirmations, invoices), account security alerts, and support responses
  • Marketing: Sending newsletters, promotional offers, and product updates (only with your explicit consent; you can opt out at any time)
  • Compliance: Meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing our Terms of Service
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity

We process your data on the legal bases of: (i) performance of a contract; (ii) compliance with legal obligations; (iii) our legitimate business interests; and (iv) your consent where obtained.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 Within the Platform

  • Advertisers' campaign details (excluding sensitive personal data) are shared with the relevant media owner to fulfill a booking
  • Media owners' listing information (name, location, asset specs) is visible to verified advertisers on the Platform

4.2 Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party vendors to help us operate, including:

  • Payment processors (e.g., Paystack, Flutterwave) — for transaction processing
  • Cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS, DigitalOcean) — for data storage and infrastructure
  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics) — for usage analysis
  • Email service providers — for transactional and marketing emails
  • Customer support tools — for managing support tickets

All service providers are contractually bound to process data only as directed by Advers and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Advers, our users, or the public.

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (e.g., local storage, pixel tags) to operate and improve our Platform. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function, such as session management and security tokens. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your settings such as dark/light theme preference and language selection.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (e.g., Google Analytics). These are anonymized.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements on third-party platforms. Only active if you have consented.

You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect Platform functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services to you. Specifically:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained for the duration of your account's existence plus 2 years after closure
  • Financial Records: Transaction records are retained for 7 years to comply with Nigerian financial regulations
  • Campaign Creatives: Retained for 1 year after campaign completion, then securely deleted unless you request earlier deletion
  • Contact Form Messages: Retained for 2 years for customer service purposes

When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it so it can no longer be associated with you.

7. Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement the following technical and organizational measures:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit between your browser and our servers
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data stored at rest
  • Secure, hashed storage of passwords using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt)
  • Regular security audits, vulnerability assessments, and penetration testing
  • Role-based access controls limiting employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Multi-factor authentication options for all user accounts

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at security@advers.ng.

8. Your Rights

As a user of the Advers Platform, you have the following rights regarding your personal data, in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to the processing of your data for marketing purposes at any time
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent previously given without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to privacy@advers.ng with proof of identity. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been misused.

9. Children's Privacy

The Advers Platform is not intended for, and does not knowingly collect personal information from, individuals under the age of 18 years. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@advers.ng.

10. Third-Party Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites, services, or applications that are not operated by Advers. We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

11. International Data Transfers

As a Nigerian company primarily serving West African markets, your data is primarily stored and processed within Nigeria. However, some of our third-party service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics) may process data in other countries. When this occurs, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Contractual data protection clauses with all international service providers
  • Transfers only to countries with adequate data protection laws or to certified providers
  • Compliance with the cross-border transfer provisions of the NDPA 2023

12. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via email to your registered address and/or a prominent notice on the Platform at least 14 days before taking effect.

Your continued use of the Platform after any policy updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy. We recommend checking this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

  • Email: privacy@advers.ng
  • Address: Data Protection Officer, Advers Technology Limited, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria
  • Support Portal: advers.ng/contact

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days and resolve all formal requests within 30 calendar days.
